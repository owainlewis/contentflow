# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Owain creates technical content and loses focus when research, scripts, media, and production progress are scattered across tools.

## Product Purpose

Make the weekly publishing plan visible and keep every planned piece connected to the work needed to finish it.

## Operating Context

Owain plans one YouTube video and seven Instagram reels per week, with LinkedIn posts derived from reels. LinkedIn newsletters, Kit emails, and carousels are also part of the desired rhythm; their exact quotas and days are undecided. Google Docs, Notion, AI chats, and Frame.io currently hold the work. Reusable B-roll is camera footage of Owain, the workspace, and everyday activities.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The current app supports YouTube, LinkedIn posts and newsletters, carousels, X, Instagram, TikTok, email, and Substack records, a weekly matrix with saved workspace targets, a calendar, autosave, and API/CLI access. Uploaded files are currently previews only. Content currently expires after 56 days; changing retention and implementing a media library are separate work. This redesign must preserve existing content, editor behavior, authentication, and safe mutation retries.

## Product Principles

- Start with the week and make the remaining work visible.
- Keep planning distinct from being ready and being published.
- Let people schedule existing ideas as easily as creating new ones.
- Keep writing focused and return easily to the plan.
- Give reusable media a lasting identity independent of publication.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository, existing interaction tests, and the user's workflow descriptions. Local browser verification uses an isolated database and clearly named test content.
